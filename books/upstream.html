The story

Upstream is a beautifully illustrated wildlife journey that invites young readers to join two determined salmon on an epic migration. From the wide-open ocean to winding rivers, these brave fish travel hundreds of miles upstream to return to the exact place they were born.

Along the way, they face rushing rapids, tricky human-made obstacles, and hungry wildlife. This unforgettable adventure brims with perseverance, courage, and determination. Can they overcome raging rapids, hungry predators, and countless obstacles to reach their home and begin a family?

Ideal for anyone who loves marine-life stories and animal journeys. Upstream blends action and heart. Its captivating illustrations make it an ideal read-aloud for classrooms, bedtime, or any child who loves ecology-themed stories. Beneath the adventure lies a touching allegory of family — the love, sacrifice, and protection parents give to help their children grow strong and independent.

A picture book for children who enjoy animal friendship and family values, Upstream inspires wonder, empathy, and a deeper appreciation for the natural world.
