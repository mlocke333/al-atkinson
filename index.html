Get to know the author

Veterinarian. Actor. Furniture maker. Storyteller.

Al has been engaged in the arts as a SAG actor for 25 years, part of two professional improv groups, a nature lover, avid outdoorsman, and a lifeguard. He also gets warm fuzzies making furniture for the home.

As a veterinarian, he has had a menagerie of pets over the years — reptiles, dogs, cats, birds, guinea pigs, and a chinchilla. Oh, and he appeared with David Letterman on “Stupid Human Tricks” in 1990.

A Colorado home base

Al spends most of his time in Colorado with his wife of over 45 years. When he’s not writing, you might find him skiing, backpacking, lifeguarding at the local pool — or negotiating with his two Labradors about what does and does not count as a chew toy.
